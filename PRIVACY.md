# jstudy privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

jstudy is a Japanese vocabulary app made by Daniel Power. It has no accounts,
no ads and no analytics, and the developer receives none of your data.

## What stays on your device

Your cards, review history, settings, stories and backups are stored on your
device only. Android's own backup to your Google account may include them,
except your Claude API key, which is left out. Backup files you export are
saved where you choose.

## What leaves your device, and only when you ask

- **Stories.** When you ask for a story, a sequel or a rewrite, jstudy sends a
  request to Anthropic's Claude API (api.anthropic.com) using the API key you
  entered. The request contains your story prompt and settings, your level, a
  list of words you know or are learning, and the story so far for sequels.
  Anthropic handles it under its own privacy policy:
  https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- **Renshuu import.** When you import from Renshuu, jstudy sends the Renshuu
  API key you enter to api.renshuu.org and downloads your studied vocabulary.
  The key isn't saved. Renshuu's own privacy policy applies.

Both features are optional, and everything else works without them. Requests
are sent over HTTPS.

## Your API key

Your Claude API key is stored on your device, is sent only to Anthropic, and
isn't included in Android backups or in exported backup files. "Reset
everything" in Settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

## Reporting a story

"Report this story" at the end of a story opens an email to the developer with
the story in it. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

## Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device.

## Children

jstudy isn't directed at children under 13.
