# Jiwa Jiwa privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Jiwa Jiwa is a Japanese vocabulary app made by Daniel Power. It has no ads and
no analytics. The developer receives no data from it.

## What stays on your device

Your cards, review history, settings, stories and backups are stored on your
device only. Android's own backup to your Google account may include them.
Backup files you export are saved where you choose.

## What leaves your device, and only when you ask

- **Renshuu import.** When you import from Renshuu, Jiwa Jiwa sends the Renshuu
  API key you enter to api.renshuu.org and downloads your studied vocabulary.
  The key isn't saved. Renshuu's own privacy policy applies.

It's optional, and everything else works without it. Requests are sent over
HTTPS.

## Story audio

The stories' recorded voices are downloaded from DigitalOcean Spaces as you
open a chapter, and kept on your device. The request names only the
recording it wants, so it says which sentences you're about to read, and
like any download it shows the server your IP address. Nothing about you or
your progress is sent. On mobile data Jiwa Jiwa asks before downloading,
unless you've turned that off in Settings. Without the recordings, stories
are read in your phone's own voice.

## Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device.

## Children

Jiwa Jiwa isn't directed at children under 13.
