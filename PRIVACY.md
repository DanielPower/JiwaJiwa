# Jiwa Jiwa privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Jiwa Jiwa is a Japanese vocabulary app made by Daniel Power. It has no ads and
no analytics. The only data the developer receives is what the optional stories
feature needs, described below.

## What stays on your device

Your cards, review history, settings, stories and backups are stored on your
device only. Android's own backup to your Google account may include them,
except your stories sign-in, which is left out. Backup files you export are
saved where you choose.

## What leaves your device, and only when you ask

- **Stories.** Stories need you to sign in with Google. Signing in sends your
  Google account's ID, email address and name to Jiwa Jiwa's story server
  (api.jiwajiwa.app), run by the developer. When you ask for a story, a sequel
  or a rewrite, Jiwa Jiwa sends the request to that server, which passes it to
  Anthropic's Claude API. The request contains your story prompt and settings,
  your level, a list of words you know or are learning, and the story so far
  for sequels. The story server keeps your Google account ID and email address,
  and how many story requests you made and how big they were in the last two
  days, to limit each account to a fair share. It doesn't keep your requests
  or stories. Anthropic handles requests under its own privacy policy:
  https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- **Renshuu import.** When you import from Renshuu, Jiwa Jiwa sends the Renshuu
  API key you enter to api.renshuu.org and downloads your studied vocabulary.
  The key isn't saved. Renshuu's own privacy policy applies.

Both features are optional, and everything else works without them. Requests
are sent over HTTPS.

## Your stories sign-in

Signing in keeps a session on your device, which isn't included in Android
backups or in exported backup files. "Sign out" or "Reset everything" in
Settings, or uninstalling the app, removes it. To have your account deleted
from the story server, email the address below.

## Reporting a story

"Report this story" at the end of a story opens an email to the developer with
the story in it. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

## Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device.

## Children

Jiwa Jiwa isn't directed at children under 13.
