# Miho's Manga

Written by `./gradlew -PcoreOnly=true :core:stories` from the chapter files here. Don't edit it by hand.

A magazine contest, a heroine who looks a lot like Yuki, and a deadline on the last day of summer.

- Id `mihos-manga`, 12 chapters.
- Follows `the-band/mihos-notebook`.

## Cast

Everyone met by the end, in the chapter that introduces them.

- **アレックス** (`arrival/im-here`): from Canada, in Japan for a year
- **ユキ** (`arrival/yuki`): lives in the share house, makes coffee, plays bass
- **店長** (`ramen-night/the-manager`): runs the ramen shop where Alex works
- **ケンタ** (`share-house/morning`): the room next door, games all night
- **ミホ** (`share-house/the-manga-shop`): works at the manga shop, has recommendations
- **母** (`share-house/a-call-from-home`): Alex's mom, back in Canada
- **ソラ** (`new-housemate/the-new-guy`): takes Alex's old room; 19, from Hokkaido, starting university
- **ダイチ** (`the-band/the-drummer`): drummer Yuki meets at the live house; loud and cheerful
- **リン** (`the-band/the-guitarist`): guitarist; cool, says little, kinder than she looks
- **佐藤** (`mihos-manga/waiting`): editor at the manga magazine; Miho met her at the publisher

## Chapters

### 1. The contest (`mihos-manga/the-contest`)

Miho finds a manga contest in a new magazine at the shop, and goes straight to the studio to show Yuki and tell the band she's entering with a manga about them.

- 113 words; 3 new of 3 allowed.
- Teaches ～に行く / へ (`ni-iku`).
- New: 雑誌 (ざっし, N5), コンテスト (N1), 見せる (みせる, N5).

### 2. The heroine (`mihos-manga/the-heroine`)

Over morning coffee Miho shows Yuki and Sora her heroine, a girl who sings and plays bass and is definitely not Yuki; Kenta asks about the deadline.

- 117 words; 3 new of 3 allowed.
- New: 話 (はなし, N5), 主人公 (しゅじんこう, N1), 締め切り (しめきり, N2).

### 3. The publisher (`mihos-manga/the-publisher`)

Miho and Sora take the train to the magazine's publisher to see pages by real pros on show; a woman there asks about Miho's manga and says she'll look forward to it.

- 109 words; 3 new of 3 allowed.
- New: 出版社 (しゅっぱんしゃ, not JLPT), プロ (N3), 電車 (でんしゃ, N5).

### 4. The pros' pages (`mihos-manga/the-pros-pages`)

Miho puts her page next to a pro's and only sees how bad hers is; Yuki says the pro draws better, but she likes Miho's more, because you can hear it.

- 110 words; 3 new of 3 allowed.
- Teaches より / ～のほうが (`yori`).
- New: 線 (せん, N4), 下手 (へた, N5), どっち (N5).

### 5. No time (`mihos-manga/no-time`)

Between shifts at the shop Miho has only a few pages done; Kenta, who knows deadlines, tells her to draw the whole story rough first, and thinks it may beat his game's.

- 113 words; 3 new of 3 allowed.
- New: 時間 (じかん, N5), ページ (N5), 少し (すこし, N5).

### 6. Drawing the band (`mihos-manga/drawing-the-band`)

Miho goes to the studio to draw the band as they play; Rin says the heroine's face is wrong, because on stage Yuki always smiles, and Daichi wants to look cooler.

- 118 words; 3 new of 3 allowed.
- New: 前 (まえ, N5), 顔 (かお, not JLPT), もっと (N5).

### 7. When I was a kid (`mihos-manga/when-i-was-a-kid`)

Drawing late in Yuki's room, Miho tells her how she started: a quiet kid alone at home with her mom's manga, a dream she put away, and the night she saw Yuki's band.

- 136 words; 4 new of 4 allowed.
- Teaches ～とき (`toki`).
- New: 子供 (こども, N5), 家 (いえ, N5), 夢 (ゆめ, N4), マンガ家 (まんがか, not JLPT).

### 8. Old notebooks (`mihos-manga/old-notebooks`)

On her day off Miho goes home to her mom's to look at her childhood notebooks, and finds the first manga she ever drew: a girl with a guitar who sings, and a band.

- 134 words; 3 new of 4 allowed.
- New: 昔 (むかし, N4), 全部 (ぜんぶ, N5), 初めて (はじめて, N5).

### 9. The ending (`mihos-manga/the-ending`)

Miho can't get her manga's last page right and asks the band; Daichi wants the heroine to win, Rin says she doesn't have to, and Yuki remembers her first time on stage.

- 140 words; 3 new of 4 allowed.
- New: そして (N5), 大事 (だいじ, N4), 心配 (しんぱい, N4).

### 10. The deadline (`mihos-manga/the-deadline`)

The last three days: drawing before work, after work and before bed; Yuki and Sora come to help, make her eat, and at dawn on the last day of summer they send it off from the konbini.

- 147 words; 3 new of 4 allowed.
- Teaches ～前に / ～た後で / ～てから (`mae-ni`).
- New: あと (N5), 寝る (ねる, N5), 早い (はやい, N5).

### 11. Waiting (`mihos-manga/waiting`)

Summer turns to autumn and Miho waits for the results without drawing, until Kenta tells her to start the next one; then the woman from the publisher calls: she's an editor, and she's read it.

- 171 words; 2 new of 5 allowed.
- New: 結果 (けっか, N3), 編集者 (へんしゅうしゃ, not JLPT).

### 12. Honourable mention (`mihos-manga/honourable-mention`)

Back at the publisher with Yuki, Miho learns her manga got an honourable mention; Sato liked its story best, cried at the last page, and gives Miho her card.

- 172 words; 4 new of 5 allowed.
- New: 佳作 (かさく, not JLPT), おめでとうございます (not JLPT), 泣く (なく, N4), 名刺 (めいし, N2).
