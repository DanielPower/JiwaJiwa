# Yuki's Song

Written by `./gradlew -PcoreOnly=true :core:stories` from the chapter files here. Don't edit it by hand.

A real live house, flyers and a poster, a song started for someone far away, and a winter show where nothing goes to plan.

- Id `yukis-song`, 16 chapters.
- Follows The Band (`the-band`), Kenta's First Job (`kentas-first-job`), Messages from Canada (`messages-from-canada`).
